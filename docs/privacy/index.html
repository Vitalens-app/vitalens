Vitalens Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Vitalens keeps your health information on your phone. There is no account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We never see, sell, or share your data.

What Vitalens stores

Everything below is saved only on your device, inside the app’s private storage:

  • Scans: photos you take or pick, the areas you mark, the body parts you choose, pain level, symptom answers, possible causes shown, and your notes.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: age, sex at birth, gender, height, weight, units, and medications.
  • Daily check-ins, reminder times, recent body-part choices, and app settings.
  • Your PIN, if you set one. It is kept in the iOS Keychain / Android Keystore.

What Vitalens does not do

Vitalens has no account and no server. It does not send your data anywhere.

It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Photos are analyzed on the phone only. Body-part suggestions run on the device and are never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Camera and Photos: only so you can take or pick a photo to mark. Vitalens saves to your photo library only when you tap Save to library.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are scheduled on the phone.

You can turn any permission off in your phone’s Settings at any time.

When data leaves the app

Only when you choose to. Sharing a PDF, CSV, or backup file uses your phone’s share sheet, and you decide where it goes.

“Learn more” and source links open MedlinePlus (medlineplus.gov) or other health sites in a browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your phone’s own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include app data, depending on your phone settings.

Deleting your data

Delete a scan from its detail screen; it moves to Recently deleted, where you can delete it for good. Settings → About → Delete all my data erases everything at once. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.

Age

Vitalens is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly let children under 13 use the app, and because nothing is sent to us, we never receive information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Vitalens gives information, not a diagnosis. It is not a medical device. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
