Information only

Vitalens provides health-related information for learning and preparation for medical visits. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for a doctor, pharmacist, or emergency services. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Subscriptions

Some features require a paid subscription (Vitalens Plus, Pro, or Ultimate). Prices are shown in the app before you purchase.

  • Subscriptions auto-renew monthly until you cancel.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Cancel anytime in your device subscription settings (on iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions).
  • Use Restore purchases in the app if you reinstall or change devices.

Free features—including emergency checks, basic results, and basic PDF summaries—remain available without a subscription.

Acceptable use

Do not use Vitalens to harm yourself or others, or to rely on it as your only source of care for serious symptoms.
